Before you call

Storm damage questions

Still have a question? Call (425) 501-4160 and talk to the crew.

What should I do if a tree falls on my house?

Get everyone out of the rooms under it and stay away from the tree. If any wires are down or touching it, treat them as live, keep back and call your power company. Then call us at (425) 501-4160. Take photos of the damage before anything is moved, because your insurance company will want them.

Do you work with insurance?

Yes. We work with insurance companies to streamline the claims process. We photograph the damage, write up the work, and give you a written estimate your adjuster can review. Call your agent early too, because coverage for tree removal depends on your policy and on what the tree hit.

Is a leaning tree dangerous?

It depends on when it started leaning. A tree that has leaned the same way for years has usually grown to balance it. A tree that started leaning after a storm, or has soil lifting or cracking on one side of the base, can fail with little warning. Keep people and cars clear of it and have it looked at quickly.

Do you haul away the debris?

Yes. Cleanup is part of the job. We cut up the trunk and limbs, haul the logs and brush off your property, and clean up the yard. The wood and green waste we remove is recycled rather than sent to a landfill.

Are you available nights and weekends?

Yes. Storm damage doesn't keep business hours, so we answer the phone 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call (425) 501-4160 and tell us what came down and where. If it's safe to wait for daylight, we'll tell you. If it isn't, we'll talk through the next steps right away.
