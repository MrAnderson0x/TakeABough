Tree removal questions

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree's size, how easy it is to reach, and what's close to it. A tall fir beside a house takes more rigging and time than a maple in an open yard. We look at the job in person and give you a free written estimate. If anything changes once we start, we talk to you before the price does.

Can you remove a tree right next to my house?

Yes. Tight removals are the work we're set up for. Instead of felling the whole tree, we climb it, cut it in sections and lower each piece by rope, away from your roof, gutters and fence. Where there's open ground, we use controlled felling to put the tree exactly where we planned.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree?

Sometimes. Many cities in Snohomish and King County regulate removing large trees, especially near streams, wetlands or steep slopes, and the rules change from city to city. Check with your city before the work date. When we come out for the estimate, we can tell you what to ask.

How fast can you come out?

Call us and we'll set a time for your free estimate. If a tree is cracked, leaning toward the house or hung up in another tree, tell us on the call so we can treat it as urgent. For a tree that came down in a storm, we answer the phone 24/7.

Are you insured?

Yes. Take a Bough is licensed, bonded and insured, with general liability and workers' compensation coverage. That matters more than most people expect, because an injury on your property involving an uninsured crew can become your problem. Ask any tree service for proof of insurance before the work starts.
