Privacy policy

Effective November 2026

What we collect

When you request an estimate, we collect what you type into the form: your name, phone number, email, address or city, the service you need, a description of the job, and any photos you attach. If you call us, we may see your phone number and keep a record of the call.

How we use it

We use it to contact you about your request, look at the job and give you an estimate. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone except the services that deliver our email and run this website.

Analytics and advertising

This site uses cookies and similar tools, including Google Analytics and Google Ads, to count visits and see which ads lead to calls and requests. Phone numbers on this site may be tracking numbers that forward to our main line, and calls may be recorded for quality. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Questions

Email quote@takeabough.com or call (425) 501-4160, and we'll answer, update or delete your information.
