Snuggies the Cat

Chief Morale Officer
All Departments

Snuggies has been the heart and soul of Take A Bough since wandering onto a job site three years ago and refusing to leave. As our unofficial Chief Morale Officer, Snuggies greets every crew member at the start of each shift, supervises stump grinding from a safe distance, and has never once missed a lunch break. This month, Snuggies is being recognized for outstanding nap consistency, flawless attendance at team meetings, and an uncanny ability to sit on the exact paperwork you need.
