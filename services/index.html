Our Services

Comprehensive tree care solutions backed by 25+ years of Pacific Northwest experience. From routine pruning to emergency storm response, we do it all.

Tree Removal

When a tree becomes a safety hazard, outgrows its space, or simply needs to come down, our crew handles the entire process with precision and care. We use rigging techniques, crane-assisted removal, and sectional dismantling to safely remove trees of any size — even in tight spaces near structures and power lines.

  • Hazardous and dead tree removal
  • Crane-assisted removal for large or difficult trees
  • Sectional dismantling near structures and utilities
  • Complete debris cleanup and haul-away
  • Permits and utility coordination when required

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Proper pruning promotes healthy growth, prevents disease, and keeps your trees looking their best. Our ISA-trained arborists follow ANSI A300 pruning standards to ensure every cut is made with purpose — improving structure, clearance, and aesthetics without compromising the tree's health.

  • Crown thinning and cleaning
  • Deadwood and hazard limb removal
  • Canopy raising and shaping
  • Vista pruning for views and light
  • Fruit tree and ornamental pruning

Stump Grinding

After a tree is removed, the stump doesn't have to stay. Our commercial-grade stump grinders pulverize stumps below grade level, leaving the area ready for replanting, landscaping, or construction. We handle everything from single stumps to large-scale clearing projects.

  • Below-grade grinding (6-12 inches below surface)
  • Surface root removal
  • Grindings cleanup or backfill
  • Site preparation for new plantings
  • Multi-stump and lot clearing pricing available

Emergency Storm Damage

Pacific Northwest storms can bring down trees and limbs without warning. Our emergency crews are on call 24/7 to respond to fallen trees on homes, vehicles, driveways, and power lines. We work directly with insurance companies and coordinate with local utilities to get your property safe and clear as fast as possible.

  • 24/7 rapid response, including weekends and holidays
  • Fallen tree removal from structures and vehicles
  • Leaning and hanging limb stabilization
  • Insurance documentation and claims assistance
  • Utility company coordination

Land Clearing

Whether you're building a home, developing a commercial lot, or reclaiming overgrown property, our land clearing services prepare your site efficiently and responsibly. We offer selective clearing to preserve specimen trees and can work within environmental and permitting constraints.

  • Residential and commercial lot clearing
  • Brush and undergrowth removal
  • Selective clearing to preserve key trees
  • Grading preparation and debris hauling
  • Habitat-sensitive and permit-compliant approaches

Arborist Consultations

Not sure if a tree needs to come down? Worried about a disease spreading to nearby trees? Our certified arborists provide professional health assessments, risk evaluations, and management plans that give you the information you need to make the right decision for your property.

  • Tree health and disease diagnosis
  • Risk and hazard assessments
  • Planting and species selection recommendations
  • Construction impact evaluations
  • Written arborist reports for permits and insurance
