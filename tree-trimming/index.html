Before you call

Tree trimming questions

Still have a question? Call (425) 501-4160 and talk to the crew.

When is the best time to prune trees?

For most leafy trees, late winter is best, while the tree is dormant. You can see the branch structure, the cuts close as growth starts in spring, and the tree is under less stress. Dead, broken or hazardous limbs are different: take those out any time of year, and ideally before winter storms.

How often should trees be trimmed?

Mature trees usually need pruning every three to five years. Young trees do better with lighter pruning more often, which sets up strong structure early. Fast growers near a roof, driveway or power line may need attention sooner. At the estimate we'll tell you what your trees need, including when to leave them alone.

Will trimming hurt my tree?

Not when it's done right. Proper cuts are made just outside the branch collar so the wound closes, and only a limited share of the living crown comes off in one visit. What hurts trees is topping. Cutting the top off leaves weak regrowth and invites decay, so it's worth avoiding.

Can you trim near power lines?

Yes, for the line that runs from the pole to your house. We work at a safe distance and can coordinate with your utility when that line needs to be dropped or cleared first. Lines along the street belong to the utility, so call them for those. Never cut limbs that are touching a line yourself.

Do you haul away the branches?

Yes. Cleanup is included in the price. We chip or haul off the branches and debris and rake up before we leave, so the only thing missing from your yard is the limbs. The wood and green waste we remove is recycled.
